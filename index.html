Questions about the books

Who is Ornella Vee?

Ornella Vee is a self-published romance author who writes contemporary and dark romance novels. She is the author of The List and The Risk, and her mafia romance Roman is due in January 2027. By day she works in corporate strategy, PR and digital marketing in Dubai.

What kind of romance books does Ornella Vee write?

Ornella Vee writes contemporary romance and dark romance with morally gray men, fierce heroines and slow-burn tension. Her books include second chance romances and rockstar romances.

Which Ornella Vee book is a rockstar romance?

The Risk is a second chance rockstar romance. A single mom and kindergarten teacher has raised her daughter alone for eight years, since her rockstar ex, Declan Evans, chose his career over the baby. When he returns at her brother's wedding wanting to know his daughter, everything is at risk.

Is The List a second chance romance?

Yes. The List, from The Redemption Series, is a second chance and celebrity crush romance. A woman with a broken marriage starts over in a new country with her young child and a bucket list, and ends up working alongside her childhood celebrity crush.

Where can I buy Ornella Vee's books?

Both The List and The Risk are available as Kindle books on Amazon, and you can add them to your shelf and read reader reviews on Goodreads. Links are in the Books section of this page.

What is Ornella Vee's next book?

Roman is Ornella Vee's upcoming mafia romance, releasing in January 2027. It starts with a runaway bride who flees her wedding straight into the arms of a dangerous stranger: one night, no names, no consequences.

Meet Ornella Vee: The Mind Behind the Mayhem and the Romance

By day, she navigates the sharp-suited world of corporate strategy, PR, and digital marketing in Dubai. By night (and during every stolen moment in between), Ornella Vee trades analytics for angst, crafting contemporary and dark romance novels that keep readers up way past their bedtime.

As the self-published author of The List and The Risk, with a dangerously addictive mafia romance hitting shelves in January 2027, Ornella specializes in morally gray men, fierce heroines, and the kind of slow-burn tension that practically melts your Kindle.

When she isn’t plotting fictional heartbreaks or chasing deadlines, you can usually find her fueled by Dunkin’ iced coffee, indulging in a shameless fast-food run, or planning her next escape to the beaches of Sri Lanka. Whether she’s painting tote bags on a Sunday afternoon or blasting classic rock and Billie Eilish on repeat, Ornella believes life is best lived with a little bit of edge, a lot of heart, and zero regrets.

Quick Facts About Ornella

  • Current obsessions: Morally gray anti-heroes, spicy chicken sandwiches, and ocean views.
  • Writing fuel: Iced coffee, classic rock playlists, and quiet mornings before the 7:00 AM grind.
  • When she’s not writing: She’s being a mom, traveling back to Sri Lanka, or getting lost in a steamy romance novel of her own.
